02 / DEVICE CONCEPT

Hydrogen fuel-cell diver sled

CONCEPT DESIGN

Power-system configuration

A prone support platform combining metal-hydride hydrogen storage, independent oxygen supply and a PEM fuel-cell–battery power system.

H1
Metal-hydride hydrogen cartridges
F1
PEM fuel-cell module
B1
Buffer battery & DC power module
O1
Independent stored oxygen

Bay sequence, aft → front: H1 / F1 / B1 / O1.

Twin aft drives, underside trim units and sliding body supports are shown across the available views.

Concept design · engineering validation pending.

Application concepts

Diver-assisted mobility with task-specific observation, imaging and payload options.

Shared hydrogen fuel-cell power platform with task-specific payloads.
