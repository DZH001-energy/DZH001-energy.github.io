Sources & photo credits.

CEAISE Lab (Clean Energy & Artificial Intelligence Systems Engineering) is a research showcase prepared for Dr. Zhihua Deng, Dr. Bin Miao and Prof. Siew Hwa Chan, Fuel Cell Lab, Nanyang Technological University. The site theme is an independent presentation identity.

Team portraits

  • Dr. Zhihua Deng: existing author-supplied professional portrait from the local personal website.
  • Dr. Bin Miao: existing local copy of the portrait labelled “Dr Miao Bin” on the NTU Corporate Laboratory people page.
  • Prof. Siew Hwa Chan: existing local copy of the portrait from the ERI@N people page. The name follows the public NTU spelling “Siew Hwa Chan”.

The portraits have not been synthetically regenerated. Website frames use CSS cropping only.

Project images

The diver-sled exterior, cutaway and exploded images are AI-generated concept illustrations based on the supplied exterior reference and the earlier internal layout. They communicate design intent, rather than verified projections from a manufacturing CAD model. The functional routing diagram follows the same longitudinal H1 / F1 / B1 / O1 bay sequence.

Research recordings

All four videos are copied from the author’s local computer. Poster images are actual frames extracted from the corresponding video. The originals and on-screen coauthor credits are preserved. The displayed team section does not replace the fuller author lists visible in individual recordings.

  • Hybrid hydrogen UAV: the latest locally available cinematic edit, with original English captions.
  • Green power to hydrogen: the supplied project’s green-power / AEM alkaline electrolyser simulation recording. It is identified as AEM rather than a conventional diaphragm electrolyser.
  • Robotic fuel-cell laboratory: the latest local professional edit, with original English captions; edited and accelerated sections remain as originally presented.
  • Ammonia cracking + SOFC: the local 90-second heat-integrated power-system replay, with English captions converted from the existing SRT file.

These recordings demonstrate research software and numerical design models. They do not establish physical flight performance, prototype measurements, calibrated autonomous robot operation or equipment safety.
